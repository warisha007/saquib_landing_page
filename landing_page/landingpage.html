About Me

A People Developer and Learning Facilitator

I've been in the learning industry for 15 years, from co-founding a training company called Torque to organising Pakistan's First Learning Festival under the auspices of Trainers' Resource Group (TRG), a training fraternity group that I managed for over 4 years.

I am an expert in professional skills including leadership, communication, problem-solving, emotional intelligence, critical thinking, conflict management, and teamwork. I travelled to Sri Lanka as the only Pakistani sent by the British Council Pakistan to train Sri Lankan and Bangladeshi faculty in Leadership and Community Engagement programmes, in addition to training Pakistani faculty from Karachi to Gilgit Baltistan on the same programme.

I've also taken coaching courses at the Coaching Academy in London and worked with organisations including the British Council, Willows International, the World Bank's CLICK Project, and IBA Karachi.

I consider myself a People Developer, with a passion for empowering others to reach their full potential and goals by coaching individuals to focus on their strengths, address their weaknesses, and build their confidence. My training sessions are interactive and engaging, using facilitation, role-playing, case studies, simulation games, and group discussions rather than relying on excessive PowerPoint slides.
